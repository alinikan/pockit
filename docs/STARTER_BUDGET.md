# How Pockit builds a first budget

Pockit's setup creates a draft, not a claim about what any person _should_ spend. It uses Canadian dollars and does not force the total under income. If draft costs exceed expected pay, the app shows that gap so the user can review their own numbers.

## 2026 Vancouver examples

| Category                          |   Starting amount | Basis                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rent, if no amount is entered     |  $2,154 per month | Median advertised Vancouver one-bedroom rent in RentMe's August 2026 listing snapshot. This is a sample of asking rents, not the rent paid by all existing tenants or people with roommates.                                                                  |
| Public transit                    | $117.20 per month | TransLink adult one-zone monthly pass, effective July 2026. Two or three zones, discounts, and pay-as-you-go travel differ.                                                                                                                                   |
| Groceries                         |    $450 per month | A single-person planning example chosen by Pockit, **not** a measured Vancouver average. Canada's Food Price Report 2026 forecasts rising food costs and uses household-specific compositions; it does not justify treating one number as right for everyone. |
| Mortgage, if no amount is entered |                $0 | A loan payment cannot be inferred responsibly from income or location. The app asks for the actual payment.                                                                                                                                                   |
| Other categories                  |            Varies | Editable planning examples, not local market prices or financial advice. Optional categories appear only when the user's setup choices call for them.                                                                                                         |

Choosing **Car** does not automatically add a loan payment. Setup asks for a car loan or lease amount, and Pockit adds that category only when a positive amount is entered. Savings and debt categories start at $0 when no monthly goal payment was entered, with a prompt to add the user's actual amount.

Sources: [RentMe August 2026 Vancouver market snapshot](https://www.rentme.ca/market-update/vancouver-august-2026), [TransLink monthly pass fares](https://www.translink.ca/monthlypass), [Canada's Food Price Report 2026](https://www.dal.ca/sites/agri-food/research/canada-s-food-price-report-2026.html).

## What users should do

1. Enter the rent or mortgage payment they actually owe during setup when they know it.
2. Open **Budget** and tap each category marked **Example amount**. Replace it with an amount based on their bills, bank statements, and chosen spending limits.
3. Choose **Starts fresh** for costs that reset monthly, or **Rolls over** for savings and irregular expenses such as car repairs. The latter carries an unused balance into later months. A rule changed for a later month keeps earlier months' calculations intact; **This month only** changes just the selected month. Switching from fresh to rollover begins a new carried balance at that month.
4. Add transactions in **Activity**. Pockit updates Home, Budget, and Compare from the same entries. Planned amounts do not move money at a bank.

Goal payments entered during setup feed the related Savings or Debt Payments starting category. When the user changes a goal's monthly amount, a still-linked starter category updates from the current month onward. Editing that category's own amount or funding choices makes it independent, so the user can keep a different budget limit if desired. A goal remains a projection; a category amount remains a plan rather than a bank transfer.
